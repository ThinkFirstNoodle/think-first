# Think First: Noodle 🐶

**Try first, then ask the AI.** A free Mac app with a small dog named Noodle who holds back the AI's answer until you've had a go.

👉 **Website & download: [trythinkfirst.com](https://trythinkfirst.com)**

Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity something as usual. Noodle covers the answer and asks what you think first. Write a rough guess, throw it to him, and he shows the answer sorted into:

- ✅ what you got on your own
- ❌ what you missed
- 💡 what only you said

## Download
- **[Latest release](https://github.com/ThinkFirstNoodle/think-first/releases/latest)**: works with AI apps *and* AI websites in your browser
- **[Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/think-first-noodle/id6834817114)**: works with the ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini apps

Free · macOS on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · No account, no ads, no tracking. Your questions and answers never leave your Mac.
