Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Think First is made by Yunseong Kim, Republic of Korea. Contact: noodle1thinkfirst@gmail.com

Think First comes in two editions. Both keep everything on your Mac. The Mac App Store edition doesn't connect to the internet at all; the edition downloaded from this site only checks GitHub for updates.

Mac App Store edition

Think First has no accounts, analytics or ads, and we have no server. What it records stays on your Mac. We can't see it.

What stays on your Mac

Think First keeps one file on your Mac, which holds:

  • the questions it noticed you asking an AI
  • your attempts, and whether you tried first or skipped
  • the AI's answers (up to 6,000 characters each), the gap results and your review cards
  • the title of the AI window, such as a conversation's name, and when you asked
  • your settings and Noodle's name and look

The file is ~/Library/Containers/com.thinkfirst.noodle.appstore/Data/Library/Application Support/think-first/thinkfirst.json. It isn't encrypted, so anyone or any app with access to your Mac user account could read it, and your own backups, such as Time Machine, may copy it.

What the permissions are used for

  • Input Monitoring: notices when you press Enter, so it can tell when you send a message. It only learns whether a key was Enter or some other key, never which key or what you type, and it ignores keys unless an AI app is in front. It doesn't save your key presses; only the question you sent is kept.
  • Screen Recording: takes pictures of the AI chat window to see when the answer has finished and, to read your question and the AI's answer from them. Reading is done on your Mac. The pictures are held in memory and are never saved or sent.
  • Front window: checks the name and title of the window in front to tell whether it's an AI chat. It only keeps the title of AI chat windows.

You can turn these off in System Settings > Privacy & Security, though Noodle can't work without them. Pause and quiet hours stop him for a while.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing. This edition of Think First doesn't connect to the internet. Updates come through the Mac App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy. Your questions, attempts, answers and stats never leave your Mac.

AI services

Think First doesn't send anything to AI services for you. What you type into ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI goes to that company under its own terms and privacy policy, just as it would without Think First.

Deleting your data

  • Remove one question from its entry in History.
  • Delete everything with Reset in Settings.
  • Deleting the app doesn't delete your data. To remove it too, delete the think-first folder shown above.

We hold no copy, so we can't recover deleted data, and there's nothing for us to hand over or delete on request.

Children

We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children, because we don't collect your data at all. If you're too young to agree to these terms where you live, a parent or guardian should agree for you.

Changes and questions

If this changes, the new version will appear here in an update, with a new date.
