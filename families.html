Questions

Good to know.

Can my child turn it off?

Yes. Your child can stop sharing any time from the Family page, and you're told when they do. Think First is about building a habit together, not secret monitoring, so it's never hidden from them.

Will I know if they pause or quit Think First?

Yes. You get a notification on the iPhone app and an email when Think First is paused, quit, stops unexpectedly (a force quit, crash or flat battery) or loses Screen Recording. At most one email per kind every 30 minutes, and never with the question in it.

Can Think First read my child's questions?

No. They're encrypted on your child's Mac with the secret half of your pairing code, which never reaches our server. Only your child's Mac and your own devices can read them. If you lose the code, old questions can't be recovered, even by us.

What does "how close" mean?

When your child tries first, Noodle compares their attempt with the AI's answer and counts the key points they got and missed, and ideas that were only theirs. You see the counts, like "got 3 of 5 key points", never the points or the answer.

Is it the same app as Think First?

It's a separate Mac app with its own data, so a personal Think First is left untouched. It adds the Family page; everything else, including Noodle, works the same.

Does it see AI websites, or only apps?

Both: the ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity apps, and AI chat websites in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Firefox and other browsers.

Can I add more than one child?

Yes. Give each child the same pairing code. You can see everyone together, or one child at a time.

Does it cost anything?

It's free.
