        Questions

        Good to know.

        Which AI apps does it work with?

        The ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity apps for Mac, and AI chat websites in your browser: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek, Grok and others. It works in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Firefox, Brave and most other browsers.

        Why does it need Screen Recording and Accessibility?

        Accessibility lets Noodle notice when you send a question and read it from the chat box. Screen Recording lets him see when the AI has finished answering, so he can compare it with your attempt. He only looks while an AI chat is in front, and everything is read on your Mac.

        Does anything get uploaded?

        No. Your questions, attempts and answers never leave your Mac. The only time Think First goes online is to check GitHub for a new version.

        Is it really free?

        Yes. No account, no ads, no trial. Just download it and go.

        What if I just want the answer?

        Press Skip and Noodle fetches it anyway. Think First is there to make trying first a habit, not to lock you out.

        Does it work on Intel Macs or Windows?

        Not yet. Think First needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).

        How do I update it?

        You don’t have to. New versions download in the background and install the next time you restart Think First.

        How do I uninstall it?

        Quit from the Noodle menu in the menu bar, then drag Think First from Applications to the Trash. To delete your history too, remove the Think First folder in ~/Library/Application Support.
