How it works

Three steps. One good habit.

You keep asking AI as much as you like. Noodle just makes sure your own brain gets the first go.

How does a vaccine work?

Ask as usual

Type a question into your AI app and press Enter, the way you always do.

You first!

Noodle holds the answer

He sits on the reply and asks what you think. A rough guess is fine. Nobody’s marking it.

See how you did

Throw it to him and he fetches the answer, side by side with yours.

The comparison

Find out what you actually knew.

Reading an answer feels like learning. Trying first is what makes it stick. Every comparison sorts the answer into three piles, so you can see your own thinking next to the AI’s.

In a hurry? Skip, and the answer appears straight away. Skips are counted, honestly.
