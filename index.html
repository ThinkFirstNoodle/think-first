Think First: Noodle

A small dog lives on your Mac. Ask an AI something and he won't fetch the answer until you throw him your own attempt first. Then he shows you what you got and what you missed.

This is a Mac app. Open this page on your Mac to download it.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Free.

downloads

Opening it the first time

  1. Open the file and drag Think First into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the app.
  3. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.
  4. Allow the two permissions the welcome guide asks for.
Works over ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, in their apps and in the browser. Everything stays on your Mac.
